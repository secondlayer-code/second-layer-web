Questions

Straight answers.

01How long does it take?

A website takes 3 to 4 business days. A booking or ordering system takes 1 to 2 weeks, and an automation 5 to 7 business days. The NFC review card is delivered across India within 2 weeks.

02What do you need from me?

Your logo, photos, a list of services with prices, and your business details. If you do not have good photos or text, we will help you put them together.

03Who owns the website?

You do. After the final payment, the website, the domain and all the content are yours.

04Are there monthly costs?

There is no fixed monthly fee. Domain and hosting are included for the first year where specified. Third-party subscriptions, paid APIs and WhatsApp API charges are only added if your project needs them, and we agree them with you first.

05What is free, and what is paid?

The business audit is free: we check your website and Google profile and tell you what we find. Every service, including the Google Business Profile setup and NFC review card, is paid, at the prices shown on our pricing page.

06How do I pay?

50% in advance to start and 50% at launch. We share the scope, timeline and final cost before any work begins.

07You are a new studio. Why should I trust you?

Fair question. We will not show you invented reviews. This website is our first project, so judge us by it. The audit is free, and you only pay if the plan makes sense to you.
