Privacy notice

This page explains, in plain words, what we collect through this website and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you send the free audit form, we receive what you type in it:

  • Your name and business name
  • Your business type and city
  • Your WhatsApp number or email address
  • Which audit you asked for, and any message you add

We also record which page you sent the form from and, if you arrived through one of our links or ads, the campaign tag on that link. This tells us which posts are useful.

What we use it for

We use your details to prepare your audit and to contact you about it. We do not sell your details and we do not add you to a mailing list.

Where it is kept

Form requests are kept in our private records and email. Only Second Layer can see them.

What this site stores on your device

This site saves a few small settings in your browser: the price region you chose, light or dark mode, whether animations are on, and whether you have already seen the audit pop-up. It sets no advertising cookies.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the details you sent. Write to support.secondlayer@gmail.com and we will do it.
